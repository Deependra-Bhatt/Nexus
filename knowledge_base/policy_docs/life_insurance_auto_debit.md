# Life Insurance — Premium Payment Terms

Life insurance premiums are billed monthly, quarterly, or annually depending on the
policy's selected frequency. Customers may pay premiums via check, online portal
payment, or automatic debit from a linked bank account.

Auto-debit is generally permitted for life insurance policies provided:

- the linked bank account is active and in good standing;
- the account is not flagged as restricted for automatic debits;
- the customer has authorized auto-debit in writing or through the customer portal.

If a linked account is restricted, auto-debit requests will be declined until the
restriction is lifted by the bank or by Meridian's account services team.
