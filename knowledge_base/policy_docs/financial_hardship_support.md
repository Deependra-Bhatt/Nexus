# Financial Hardship Support

Customers who tell us they are struggling to pay premiums, loans or other commitments are always
referred to a trained hardship specialist. Options such as payment plans or temporary relief are
discussed only by that specialist, who reviews the customer's full circumstances. The concierge
never offers or refuses hardship options itself.
