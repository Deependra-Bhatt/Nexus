# FAQ — Setting Up Auto-Debit

**Q: Can I set up auto-debit for my insurance premium from my bank account?**

A: In most cases, yes. Auto-debit is generally permitted for life insurance policies
as long as the linked bank account is active and not restricted. Auto insurance
auto-debit is more limited and depends on policy tier. Always confirm the specific
coverage clause for your policy type before assuming auto-debit is available.
