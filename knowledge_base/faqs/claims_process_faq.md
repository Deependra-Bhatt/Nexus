# FAQ — How Insurance Claims Are Reviewed

**Q: What happens after I file a claim?**

A: Every claim is first logged and then assessed by a claims specialist. A claim shown as
"under_review" is still being assessed; no settlement decision has been made yet. Customers are
notified when the status changes. The concierge can report the current status of a claim but
cannot approve, deny or settle it.
