# FAQ — Protecting Yourself From Scams

Meridian will never ask you for a one-time passcode, PIN or online banking password, and will
never ask you to move money to a "safe account". If someone contacts you claiming to be from
Meridian and asks for these details, end the contact and tell us. Concerns about coercion, scams
or someone else using your money are handled by our safeguarding team.
