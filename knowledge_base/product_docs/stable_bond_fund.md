# Meridian Stable Bond Fund — Overview

The Meridian Stable Bond Fund is a conservative fixed-income fund that aims to preserve capital
and provide steady income. It is suitable for customers with a low, moderate or high risk
profile. The minimum initial investment is $500. Returns are not guaranteed and the value of
the fund can fall as well as rise.
