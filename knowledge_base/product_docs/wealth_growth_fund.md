# Meridian Growth Equity Fund — Overview

The Meridian Growth Equity Fund is a high-risk, high-growth-potential fund suited to
investors with a high risk tolerance and a long investment horizon. It is not
suitable for customers with a low or moderate risk profile.

The minimum initial investment is $1,000. Suitability is confirmed against the customer's
recorded risk profile before any purchase.
